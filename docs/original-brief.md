# Unitrgovina — Website Design & Build Brief

Oct 21, 2026 · @123

## Project brief

Build a fast, mobile-first, Serbian + Slovak website for Unitrgovina d.o.o. (Padina, since 1990) that turns searches for concrete, gravel, building materials, firewood and grain prices into phone calls and quote requests. It replaces unitrgovina.rs, a 10-page site with outdated and conflicting information.

**How to use this brief:**

- Copy marked as Serbian text is ready to paste into the site.
- **\[POTVRDITI: …\]** marks a fact the client must confirm. Nothing with that mark goes live.
- Every fact in the copy comes from the old site or the client; the evidence is in Unitrgovina — Old Site Audit & New Site Strategy.

**What the site must do, in order of value:**

1. Get the phone ringing: click-to-call and Viber/WhatsApp on every screen.
2. Collect quote requests for concrete and pumps, with the details sales needs to price them.
3. Rank first on Google Maps and Search for concrete, gravel, building materials and firewood around Padina and Kovačica.
4. Bring farmers back daily with the grain price board and the *Žito za zid* programme.
5. Show the business as one chain no rival can match: gravel pit → lab → concrete plant → pump → foundation.

**Audiences:**

| Who | Arrives looking for | Must find in 10 seconds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Families building or renovating | Everything for one house | One phone number, delivery, a quote form |
| Contractors | Concrete and a pump on a set day | Grades, pump lengths, mixer sizes, fast callback |
| Farms and industrial investors | Barns, halls, industrial floors | Special concretes, fibre floors, turnkey references |
| Farmers | Today's grain price | Price board with date and time, intake hours |
| Households | Firewood and coal before winter | Prices, units, delivery |

**Success after 6 months (targets to agree with the client):** quote requests and calls tracked monthly and rising; 100+ Google reviews; top 3 on Google Maps for *beton* and *šljunak* searches in Kovačica municipality; the grain price board updated every working day.

## Brand & voice

The brand promise is one chain, one responsible family firm: *Od reke do temelja. Od njive do krova.* Every page should prove a piece of that chain with a number, a place or a photo.

**Name:** "Unitrgovina" in running text; "Unitrgovina d.o.o." in the footer and legal text.

| Support line | Porodična firma iz Padine od 1990. | Rodinná firma z Padiny od roku 1990. |
| Barter programme | Žito za zid | Obilie za múr |
| Main call to action | Zatražite ponudu | Vyžiadajte si ponuku |
| Phone call to action | Pozovite nas | Zavolajte nám |

**Five key messages (use them across the site):**

1. **Ceo lanac je naš.** Sopstvena separacija u Jarkovcu, laboratorija i fabrika betona u Padini, naši mikseri i pumpe.
2. **Atest za svaki beton.** Svi betoni su atestirani i sertifikovani od strane IMS-a.
3. **Sve na jednom mestu.** Beton, šljunak, građevinski materijal, armatura, ogrev — od temelja do krova.
4. **Vaše žito vredi kao novac.** Otkup po dnevnim cenama ili zamena za materijal i ogrev.
5. **Porodična firma od 1990.** Tri prodajna mesta, sopstvena vozila, isti ljudi godinama.

**Tone:** direct, practical, local and warm — like the yard manager explaining something to a neighbour. Short sentences, real numbers, real place names. Proud of Banat and of Padina's Slovak heritage, never boastful.

**Writing rules:**

- Latin script, ekavian, đ never written as dj.
- Address the reader as *vi / vaš* in lowercase (the old site used *Vi / Vam*; lowercase reads more naturally on the web).
- Every claim carries proof: a number, a place, a certificate or a photo.
- One term per thing: *gotov beton*, *beton pumpa*, *separisani šljunak*, *iberlauf*, *fert gredice*, *ogrev*.

## Visual design direction

The look is "Banat industrial": the three materials of the brand story — river, wheat and concrete — become the palette, set with strong condensed headings, real photography and plenty of white space. It should feel like well-kept yard signage, not a corporate brochure.

### Imagery

- Real photos only, no stock: the plant, the lab, pumps at full reach, mixers in a row, the Jarkovac separation from a drone, every gravel fraction in close-up, the team by name.
- Daylight, documentary style, one consistent warm grade; people at work in shot wherever possible.
- Homepage hero: a muted video loop of a pump pour, 8 seconds or less, under 2 MB, with a still image fallback.
- Every product card gets its own photo; the old site reused one gallery on two pages.

### Shapes and components

- **Chain motif:** numbered steps joined by a line (separacija → laboratorija → fabrika → pumpa → temelj), reused on Home, Beton and O nama.
- **Strata divider:** thin horizontal bands in Beton and Pršenica between sections, like layers in a gravel pit.
- **Buttons:** primary = Pršenica fill + Grafit text; secondary = Reka outline; 4 px radius; 48 px minimum height.
- **Cards:** white, 1 px Linija border, 8 px radius, photo on top, a "from" price or spec line at the bottom.
- **Icons:** simple 1.5 px line icons (e.g. Lucide); custom icons for mixer, pump, gravel, grain and firewood in the same style.
- **Motion:** minimal — fade-in on scroll, count-up on the numbers band; respect reduced-motion settings.

### Calculators (/kalkulatori/ + embedded on product pages)

**Concrete.** Inputs: length L (m), width Š (m), thickness D (cm), reserve r (%, default set by client \[POTVRDITI\]).

```latex
V = L \cdot \check{S} \cdot \frac{D}{100} \cdot \left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)
```

Mixer trips = V ÷ 10, rounded up (10 m³ mixer). Output: *Potrebno je oko X m³ betona – Y tura miksera.* Button: *Zatražite ponudu za X m³* (pre-fills the concrete form).

**Aggregates.** Inputs: area A (m²), layer depth d (cm), fraction. Volume V = A × d ÷ 100 (m³); mass = V × bulk density of that fraction (t/m³, supplied by client per fraction \[POTVRDITI\]). Show both m³ and t.

**Blocks.** Inputs: wall area A (m²), block type. Blocks = A × blocks per m² (from product data) × (1 + waste % \[POTVRDITI\]); pallets = blocks ÷ pieces per pallet, rounded up.

All calculators show the line *Rezultat je okviran; tačnu količinu potvrđujemo u ponudi.*

## Technical & SEO spec

Any modern stack works if it meets four hard requirements: staff can edit prices from a phone, Serbian and Slovak are first-class, pages load fast on rural 4G, and every SEO item below is in place at launch.

### Stack options

| Option | Good for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WordPress + custom fields + a multilingual plugin + a light theme | Easiest handover; staff already know the editor | Plugin bloat; keep plugins few and updated |
| Astro or Next.js + headless CMS (e.g. Sanity, Strapi, Decap) | Fastest pages, cleanest code | Needs a developer for structural changes |

Either way: EU hosting, HTTPS, daily backups, staging site, admin on a non-public path with two-factor login.

### Performance budget

- Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 s, layout shift under 0.1, interaction delay under 200 ms on a mid-range phone over 4G.
- Images in WebP/AVIF with responsive sizes; hero video lazy, muted, under 2 MB, with a still image first.
- Fonts self-hosted, Latin Extended subset only, `font-display: swap`.
- Map, reviews and chat widgets load only when scrolled into view or tapped.

### On-page SEO

- Title and meta description per page as given in sections 5–11; one H1 per page.
- Canonical tag on every page; one host (www or not) and HTTPS enforced by 301.
- robots.txt and an auto-generated XML sitemap, both submitted in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Breadcrumbs with BreadcrumbList markup; Open Graph title, description and image per page.
- Descriptive Serbian file names and alt text on every image.
- 301 redirects from the old URLs (section 4) live on launch day.
- `lang="sr-Latn"` on Serbian pages, `lang="sk"` on Slovak pages.
